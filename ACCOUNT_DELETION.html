Account Deletion — StudyStreak

You can request deletion of your StudyStreak account and all associated data in either of these ways:

Option 1: In the app (fastest)

  1. 1. Open StudyStreak and sign in
  2. 2. Go to **Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account**
  3. 3. Confirm twice when prompted

Your account and all data are deleted immediately and permanently — tasks, streaks, points, badges, study session history, and leaderboard records.

Option 2: By email

If you no longer have access to the app, email [FILL IN YOUR CONTACT EMAIL] from the email address associated with your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

We will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days of receiving your request, and reply to confirm once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • All to-do items, priorities, and due dates
  • Streaks, points, and badges
  • Study session history, daily time bank, and Pending Vault records
  • Leaderboard entries, including past weeks' results
  • Referral records (both codes you shared and any you redeemed)
  • Feedback survey responses
  • Daily puzzle attempts

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.
